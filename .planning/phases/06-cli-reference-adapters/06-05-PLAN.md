<objective>
Deliver CLI-02: `stint receipts` (merged plain-language timeline marking verified vs attested) and `stint verify` (explicit chain-integrity check reporting the exact break locus). Both are thin renderers over data that already exists in `@stint/core` (`mergeTimeline`, `verifyChain`, `verifyAttestedChain`) — the CLI adds text rendering, `--json`, and exit-code mapping only, no new integrity logic.

Purpose: SC#3 — a user reads a lease's history as one timeline and can prove chain integrity from the terminal, with a tampered receipt file reported at the exact break. The five-reason enum discipline (Pitfall 9) and checkpoint-anchored verify (Pitfall 8) are the two correctness traps this plan closes.
Output: `render/timeline.ts`, `render/verify.ts`, and the `receipts`/`verify` command bodies (replacing the 06-04 stubs).
</objective>
