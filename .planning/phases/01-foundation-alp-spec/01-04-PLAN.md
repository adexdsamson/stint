| cleanup_incomplete | retry_teardown | user | tearing_down |
| cleanup_incomplete | retry_teardown | runtime | tearing_down |

Notes the prose must carry: declined and cleaned_up are terminal. The error threshold ends in failed with actor policy (LIFE-07); this supersedes the illustrative "error_threshold to revoked" row in research/ARCHITECTURE.md Pattern 4. Completion is driven by the verifier actor (LIFE-06), not runtime. activate is guarded: the presented manifest's content hash equals the consented hash, every delegated grant is acquired (delegated and hybrid), and a license is issued (hosted and hybrid); a hash mismatch at activation yields activation_failed, and on resume (runtime restart) yields runtime_failure (LIFE-03). extend is the only transition that moves expiry and requires fresh consent through the HostAdapter (LIFE-05).
