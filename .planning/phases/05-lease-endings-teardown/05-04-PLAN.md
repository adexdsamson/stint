<objective>
Harden teardown step 1 (revoke OAuth) into an honest, structural RFC 7009 revoker: determine revocation support from AS metadata (never from a response code), record `revoked | discarded_revocation_unsupported | failed` per credential, always discard the runtime's cached credential copy regardless of outcome (D-22), and make the attempt terminal so retry never re-revokes (D-23). This is the core teardown-honesty trap (Pitfall 5 / TEAR-02).

Purpose: a prompt-injected or misbehaving agent's lease must end with the runtime holding nothing usable, and the receipt must say exactly what is known — never overclaim a revocation the runtime cannot structurally confirm.
Output: `revokeCredential` (structural RFC 7009) in oauth-client.ts, vault revoke+discard methods, the real step-1 implementation, and the honesty test matrix.
</objective>

| vault -> teardown step | The refresh token never leaves the vault; the step receives only a closed RevokeResult/outcome. |
| step outcome -> receipt | No token or raw error may cross into the receipt. |
