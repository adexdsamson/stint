<objective>
Deliver publisher entitlement revocation (LIC-04): a runtime-facing revoke entry point that mirrors the provider-revocation path — `entitlement_revoked` (actor publisher) -> `revoked` -> auto-chained teardown — plus the additive `LicenseIssuer.invalidate` port and teardown step 2 (invalidate license + discard the held license so nothing can reissue it).

Purpose: a publisher pulling entitlement must end the lease and revoke credentials through the exact same honest teardown as every other end reason, and the runtime must stop being able to refresh the license by discarding custody, not by a background loop.
Output: `applyEntitlementRevocation`, `LicenseIssuer.invalidate` (+ mock), the real step-2 implementation, and the LIC-04 test.
</objective>
