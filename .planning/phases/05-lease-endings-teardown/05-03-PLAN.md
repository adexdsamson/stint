<objective>
The teardown tracer: wire one end reason end-to-end through every layer teardown touches — auto-chain begin_teardown from a terminal end state, walk the fixed 5-step orchestrator once with all steps succeeding under the per-lease serializer, persist per-step progress, and land cleaned_up with a final signed checkpoint that verifies (TEAR-01). Then the fault/resume expansion: one-pass semantics, single-step failure -> cleanup_incomplete with every outcome recorded, and idempotent retry_teardown resuming from persisted progress without ever returning to active (TEAR-04, TEAR-05 orchestrator matrix).

Purpose: prove the teardown architecture (auto-chain, reduce()-only transitions, injectable steps, incremental progress persistence, checkpoint bracketing, notify) on the agent's best early-context tokens, before hardening each step's real I/O.
Output: `teardown/{auto-chain,orchestrate,steps,progress}.ts` and the happy-path + fault-matrix tests.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| in-flight call vs teardown | Concurrent tools/call and teardown RMW on one lease must serialize (per-lease mutex). |
| step outcome -> receipt/progress | Step outcomes are a closed enum; no secret or raw data crosses into the receipt or progress record. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-03-T | Tampering | a lease left terminal-but-not-torn-down (torn state) | high | mitigate | begin_teardown auto-chains via chainTeardownIfEnded (D-18); orchestrator resumes any tearing_down/cleanup_incomplete lease. |
| T-05-03-R | Repudiation | teardown outcome not recorded on a partial failure | high | mitigate | Every step's outcome is receipted (verified chain) and persisted to teardownProgress before the next step (D-14, D-24). |
| T-05-03-D | Denial of Service | a concurrent call corrupting teardown RMW | medium | mitigate | begin_teardown + progress writes run under runInLeaseTransaction (D-30); post-active calls denied lease_not_active (Phase 4). |
| T-05-03-E | Elevation of Privilege | a lease resurrected to active via teardown | high | mitigate | Only core reduce()/TRANSITION_TABLE transitions are used; no table path goes teardown-state -> active; tests assert it (TEAR-04). |
