<objective>
Ship the ReceiptStore persistence contract as enabling work: the interface, an in-memory double, and a reusable contract-test factory in @stint/core/testing, plus the @stint/core public barrel for the receipts surface. Output: receipt-store.ts, testing.ts receipt factories, receipts barrel exports, and the contract suite.
</objective>
