<objective>
Deliver the attested chain (publisher-signed claims) as an independently-verifying chain reusing @stint/spec's trust model, and the display-only merged timeline that marks each entry verified or attested without touching either chain's integrity (RCPT-04, RCPT-05, D-07, D-10).

Purpose: A weak attested claim must never be able to borrow the verified chain's cryptographic strength; and a user-facing timeline must combine both chains for readability while carrying no integrity meaning of its own. This plan enforces both invariants in pure code.
Output: attested.ts (publisher-signature verification over the attested chain), merge.ts (display-only timeline).
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| publisher -> attested chain | Publisher-signed claims are attested (integrity, not completeness); verified against the TrustStore, never trusted as fact. |
| chain integrity -> display | The merged timeline is a display artifact; it must not be a place integrity can be laundered. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-07 | Elevation of Privilege | attested vs verified chain | high | mitigate | Two independently hash-linked, independently checkpointed chains (D-07); a weak attested claim cannot borrow a verified checkpoint's strength. |
| T-03-08 | Tampering | mergeTimeline | medium | mitigate | Merge is a pure read-side function; inputs are readonly and never mutated; ordering carries no integrity meaning (D-10). |
| T-03-09 | Spoofing | attested publisher signature | high | mitigate | Reuse @stint/spec TrustStore/kid + verifyDetached; unknown-kid and wrong-key entries are rejected. |
</threat_model>
